# Demo video plan (target 2:30, hard limit under 3:00)

## Record on the Quest 3 (best quality)

1. Quest settings → Camera → Recording: set to 1080p (or the highest available) and 30 or 60 fps. Turn on audio recording.
2. Open https://shogunace.github.io/abyss-vr/ in Quest Browser and press Enter XR. Put the controllers down.
3. Start recording: open the Meta menu (look at your palm and pinch) → Camera → **Record video**. Recording also stops from there.
4. Move your head **slowly**. Viewers get sick from fast head turns in recordings. Hold each shot 3–5 s longer than feels natural.
5. Record each beat below as its own clip so you can trim and re-take. Then copy the clips with the Meta Horizon app, or by connecting the headset over USB and opening `Oculus/VideoShots`.

Alternative: casting to a phone or PC (Meta Horizon app → Cast) and screen-recording it there gives a cleaner field of view.

## Tips

- Keep in-headset sound on: the procedural soundscape and chimes sell the depth.
- If NAMI's captions clutter a shot, use the **Guide** button to hide them.
- Use the emulator screenshots in `submission/screenshots/` for the Devpost gallery, and grab a few in-headset photos too (Meta menu → Camera → Take photo); they're sharper.
- Edit in any free editor (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Clipchamp). Upload to YouTube as Unlisted or Public and paste the link.
